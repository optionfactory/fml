tabs, async panel

Entering a panel fires the section:requested family on the tabs — the generic type, the panel's #index — and the component awaits the answers, the loading chrome covering the wait: a panel can deliver itself. Nobody listening is a plain activation, panels rendered on the server never know the event exists. The event's target is the component, so a host's own panels are told from a nested component's by e.target === e.currentTarget.

Entering a step fires the section:requested family on the wizard: the generic type, its #index, and its data-step name.
