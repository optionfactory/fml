ful disclosures and toasts

Everything here runs on the native platform: tooltips are popovers, dialogs and drawers are <dialog>s with their focus trapping and Escape handling, toasts are a live region. The library wires them, dist/ful.css paints them.

tooltips

An icon button toggling a short explanation: placement picks the side, the default is below. Light dismiss, Escape and the aria-expanded accounting come from the wiring.

the four placements, below the default
the info slot: a field carrying its own explanation VAT number the Italian VAT identifier: eleven digits, IT-prefixed. Work email reaches you during office hours; personal addresses live in the profile.

dialogs

The first one is fully declarative: any element carrying dialog-target set to the dialog's id opens it, and the localized acknowledge button closes it. The second one answers with the data-result of the button that closed it: ask() resolves with it, or null on Escape.

a declarative notice and a question answering ask() The ledger closes at 23:00 for the monthly reconciliation. Expect a read-only window of about ten minutes. the answer will appear here Once published, the entries are immutable and any correction needs a reversing entry.

drawers

The first one shows its slotted body, opened again by a dialog-target. The other two are driven through update(title, callback): the drawer opens in a loading state, then shows either the content the callback delivers or the problems a rejection carries.

one slotted, one delivered, one failing This body was slotted in the markup: update() never ran on this drawer. You should never see this: update() replaces the slotted body with the delivered content.

dialogs and drawers, delivered on open

Opening either fires section:requested on the disclosure: answer it and the content arrives under the loading chrome, every open asking again, and the delivered content can carry its own refresh() call, the drawer's update() staying its own cycle. It is per disclosure: no listener, no wait, and the slotted body shows as-is.

a dialog and a drawer feeding themselves, refreshable from within

toasts

One ful-toasts region per page. Each toast announces itself by severity, carries its own dismiss button and retires on its timer. Anything in the page can raise one through the show-toast document event, and a Failure shows its problems.
