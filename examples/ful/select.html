ful-select

Single or multiple, chips or item list, options slotted from a native select or answered by a loader. The combobox pattern walks it: arrows, Home/End, Escape, and a click anywhere on the field toggles the list. Reopening the menu marks the options already picked, and an item list carries one deletable chip per entry, templatable through the items slot.

loader-fed

The remote shape without the network: a mock loader answers after a delay, so opening shows the spinner the real endpoint would. The vocabulary is the one the applications run: src, method, revision for the cached whole-set fetch, mode="chunked" for the searched one, k-expr/l-expr to map the payload.

a cached vocabulary

revision has three states. With no revision nothing is cached. With revision="r7" the fetched vocabulary is kept in local storage under r7, and a load under another revision evicts it. With a valueless revision it is kept under whatever the registry's revision component answers, which a page states once for every select on it. A valueless one with no component defined caches nothing and warns in the console.
