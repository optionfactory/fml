### version 9.0.0

- [BRK] `ful-spinner` is a style-only tag: the glyph is the tag's own pseudo-element, the label its light-dom children — no backing element to upgrade, nothing to flash-guard, it spins the moment the stylesheet paints; `role="status"` (and any name) is the author's to carry now, the `.ful-spinner-wrapper`/`.ful-spinner-icon` hooks and the `Spinner` export are gone (a self-registering page drops its `defineElement('ful-spinner', …)` line), and the `backdrop` page-loading shape keeps its card — the scrim covering the viewport, the glyph wearing its own padded card on top, the scroll lock while it shows — taking no label, the portaleprodotti markup working as-is
- [NEW] `.ful-button`, the opt-in button chrome: a filled primary resting on the theme's accent pair and its `.ghost` mirror (an accent outline filling on hover, the library's own ghost idiom), sized and rounded by `--ful-controls-*`, themed end to end through the `--ful-button-*` custom properties, the canon focus halo and the disabled dimming included; an anchor wears it just the same, `aria-disabled` its dimming door where no `:disabled` exists, and `.ful-button.small` is the compact step (smaller type on tighter padding, the height following); the example pages wear it instead of their own button css

- [NEW] `ful-tabs`, `ful-accordion` and `ful-wizard`: the navigation family, distilled from the homeinsurance wizard layout and its bootstrap tabs. `ful-tabs` is the tab pattern on declared structure — `<tab>` elements in the `tabs` slot pairing with the slotless panels in order — a tablist of real buttons with roving tabindex and automatic activation (arrows and `Home`/`End` walk, wrapping around), each panel a `tabpanel` named by its tab and an author-named panel keeping its id; the `active` claim (attribute, property, click, key) picks the visible panel and every move answers with a `change` event. `ful-accordion` is chrome over the native `details`/`summary` platform — semantics, keyboard and toggling included — where the `exclusive` claim names the group the platform's own way, opening one panel closing the others, and the summary's chevron rotates on open (reduced motion respected). `ful-wizard` is the homeinsurance shape: `<step>` declarations over `data-step` sections, the current step the `aria-current="step"` claim carried in lockstep by the step and its section — which section is shown follows the claim alone, done steps carry the accent, future ones stay muted but legible — with `next()`/`prev()`/`move(name)` walking (clamped at the ends, a section already carrying the claim keeps it) and each landing answering with a `change` event; the progress announces itself through the localized `wizard.progress`. The chrome anchors on the `ful-tablist`, `ful-accordion-group` and `ful-steps` style-only tags, so subclasses and foreign markup built from them keep the look. The navigation example carries the form-driven shape the applications run: each step hosting a `ful-form`, a failure pinning its problems to its own step while nothing moves, a success moving where the response says, and a field's `info` slot riding the label's line (the label inline-blocks, nothing else moving — the control group is block)
- [NEW] `ful-toasts`: a transient feedback region, distilled from two applications' toast wrappers — one region per page where each `show()` (or a `show-toast` document event carrying the same detail) stacks a toast that retires on its own timer (`timeout` attribute, five seconds by default) or its dismiss button, so concurrent messages no longer cut each other short; `severity` picks the theme and the announcement (`info` default, `success`, `warning`, and `error` announced as an alert), and a `Failure` message shows its problems' reasons. The disclosure family and the toasts are reusable the way the inputs are — a subclass overriding the template keeps the chrome, which matches the rendered structure instead of the host tags: the `ful-toast` style-only tag and whoever hosts the toasts as the region, the button before a `ful-note` (or its `.ful-tip` class) as the trigger, the `ful-note` tag (or its `.ful-note` class) as the anchored bubble, the `ful-dialog`/`ful-drawer` classes on the native dialog; the geometry and the scrim are themed through `--ful-dialog-width`, `--ful-drawer-width`, `--ful-toasts-max-width` and `--ful-backdrop-color`. The same correction is applied to the whole library: the input tag list, the select, file and radio-group chrome now anchor on whoever presents their structure (`*:has(> ful-control-group > input)`, `*:has(> ful-control-group > ful-dropdown)`, `*:has(> fieldset > ful-radio-list)`), the tables on the new `ful-table-wrapper` style-only tag, the pagination on its `ful-pagination-bar` (a `role="navigation"` style-only tag standing in for the bare nav, so generic markup cannot catch the chrome), and the filter operands moved inside a `ful-control` (the select's own shape) so they no longer share the bare-input family's chrome; only the pre-render flash guards remain tag-based
- [NEW] `ful-tooltip`, `ful-dialog` and `ful-drawer`: the disclosure family, distilled from the bootstrap-tooltip, modal and offcanvas wrappers two applications carried (an info icon toggling a popover, a centered dialog answering a question, a side drawer opening with a promise). `ful-tooltip` is a themed icon button and a native popover carrying the slotted explanation (`placement` picks the side, the popover's own Escape and light dismiss close it, the button announces its state through `aria-expanded`); `ful-dialog` is a native `<dialog>` with a header, a body and buttons, where `open()`/`ask()` resolve with the `data-result` of the button that closed it (null on Escape) and a localized acknowledge button shows when none is slotted; `ful-drawer` is the same dialog platform styled as a side panel (`placement="end|start"`), its `update(title, callback)` opening in a loading state and showing either the delivered content (the promise resolving with the content section, so a form is wired through the return value instead of a querySelector into the drawer) or the problems a rejecting callback carries, tms-offcanvas's contract without the bootstrap privates underneath; any element carrying `dialog-target` set to a dialog or drawer id opens it, clones included. The lifted family holds the library's own invariants: `update()` claims each opening (a superseded delivery is not painted, a superseded rejection still travels to its caller but owns no drawer), the dialog and the drawer answer with a `close` event (the dialog's carrying the answer), an `ask()` waiter on a dialog leaving the document while open is answered with null instead of hanging forever, the toasts' `show-toast` door is wired once per document and forwards to every live region (a second region no longer doubles every toast, a removed one stops answering), the failure reading is one helper for the drawer, the toasts and the table feedback (the problems' reasons, one per line), the drawer slides in from the inline end side in every writing direction, the wizard moves the focus to the step that just became current (the control that moved it left the document with its own section), and the visible tab panel joins the tab order right after its tab
- [BRK] ftl: `data-tpl-text` and `data-tpl-html` are gone, text and html land through the interpolations alone (`data-tpl-text="x"` becomes `>{{ x }}<`, `data-tpl-html="x"` becomes `>{{{ x }}}<`, the interpolation being a child text node instead of replacing every child, which nothing relied on), `{{{ }}}` now accepts any value the attribute form accepted (numbers and `toString`-ables are stringified, `null` and `undefined` render nothing) and `{{{{ }}}` with a non-Node reports `Expected a Node`; identifiers sharing a keyword's prefix (`nullable`, `nullish`, `falseFlag`, `truex`) parse again, a call on a missing value reports `Method missing` (by name where the grammar knows it, a bare `boom()` included) instead of crashing while building its own message, the identifier rule taking the whole word and classifying the exact keywords after it, same-named data fields behaving exactly as before; a template string is a valid dict key, evaluated like any other, `{`{k}`: 1}` building a `k`-named entry instead of the literal `"undefined"` the parts-based node used to collapse into; and `registry.ready()` is the awaitable form of `ftl:ready`, resolving right after the event and immediately when that moment already passed: an import landing after the document's DOMContentLoaded no longer strands the waiters (in the window before load included, the wait racing the two events rather than guessing from `readyState`, so it never settles early), and a last-in-document element no longer strands its upgrade on a listener that would never fire; breaking: only `text/html` scripts are parsed as slot markup, any other script type (a module included, and the `text/markdown` the wiki once suggested) now slots as the plain node it is with its source left alone, so a page using another type as a markup container moves to `text/html`, and no script source is ever innerHTML-ed again
- [BRK] httpc and client-errors: the request url is built instead of spliced, a second `?` no longer truncates the query and a `#` no longer swallows the parameters into the fragment where the server never sees them (the fragment is split off and reattached after the parameters, the query splits at its first `?` only), and `exchange()` normalizes its options before the chain sees them, so a bare call under a `CsrfTokenInterceptor` no longer crashes; `param` skips nullish entries among real values in any position, like the plural forms always did, instead of discarding the values after a leading null or appending a stringified one; an error response reports faithfully whatever went wrong with its body (not decoding as json, decoding to json that violates the `failures+json`/`problem+json` contract, or unreadable altogether) as a `GENERIC_PROBLEM` carrying the served status, instead of `"undefined failures"`, a non-array `problems`, or a `CONNECTION_PROBLEM` with status 0 mislabeling a served response; `Base64.decode` and `Hex.decode` reject invalid input (characters outside the dialect, misplaced or overlong padding, impossible lengths, non-hex characters) instead of returning plausible-looking garbage; and client-errors sends the csrf header only when the meta pair is complete, a half-configured page having sent a literal `"undefined"` token on every report, and its missing-attribute complaint is logged once per page, not once per captured failure; the csrf interceptor reads the meta pair at request time (metas landed after the client was built are honored without a rebuild) and carries the token to the page origin only, so a cross-origin request no longer ships the session token to a third party
- [BRK] ful, forms and their fields: the positional slots are the themed ones only, `before`/`after` render inside a `ful-affix` and both `ibefore`/`iafter` and the old raw unthemed passthroughs are gone; the `disabled`, `readonly` and `required` claims and the value are live doors on every field, observed on the `Field` base and composed along the inheritance chain so a post-upgrade `setAttribute` reaches the claim, the inner control and the form's extraction together, the value defaulting to the string mapper and every field with its own vocabulary overriding it (`value:bool`, `value:csvm`, `value:json`), and a custom field's `form.reset()` no longer crashes on the missing value mapper: the base's inert pair absorbs the write, and an attribute write arriving between an element's upgrade and its render lands in the observed snapshot and is applied by the render (a `ful-select` whose prefetch is in flight keeps a claim, or a `value`, made while it loaded), the properties being the post-render live door alone; the claims hold on the chrome too: a disabled or readonly `ful-input-file` accepts no drop, dismisses no removal and opens no picker (readonly freezes the whole control group, a file input having no native freeze), and a claim landing on a `ful-select` while its dropdown is open drops the pick and closes the list; `ful-input-file` honors the whole `accept` vocabulary (mime types with parameters ignored, and `image/*`-style families, not extensions alone); a drop carrying more than one file on a single-file field is rejected whole, as the native input's own drop is, and a dropped directory is ignored; Enter submits through a submitter the form actually owns, and never submits from a file field, whose Enter opens the picker as a native file input's always did; a malformed mask is warned once and then ignored, instead of throwing on every keystroke; a `FIELD_ERROR` carrying no context lands in the banner instead of crashing the display; a `ful-radio-group` assigned an unknown key clears its selection, like a null assignment and like the select's unknown keys, instead of leaving the stale radio answering; overlapping names (`a` alongside `a.b`) no longer abort the extraction with a TypeError, the later, more specific name winning as the reverse order always did; a submit button that joined the form mid-spin keeps its authored state when the spinner restores (the saved ones restore as before), and an empty table paginates as one empty page (`Page 1 of 1`, arrows dead, reload alive) instead of a phantom page one of zero; `form.reset()` restores every field through its own mapper and value semantics; and `Field` is exported from the ful entry, so a custom field inherits form integration, error display and the claim protocols while owning its template and value semantics
